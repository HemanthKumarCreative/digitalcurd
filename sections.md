# Home Page Sections

Based on the structure of the `src/components/home/HomeContent.tsx` file and its child components, here are all the sections currently available on the home page:

1. **Hero Section**
   - The main header section ("Transform Your Business for the AI Era") with a primary call to action ("Schedule a Call") and a brief preview of client logos.
   
2. **Awards/Services Marquee (`running-bar`)**
   - An auto-scrolling ticker bar highlighting key services (e.g., AI Business Solutions, Digital Marketing, Shopify & Ecommerce, etc.).

3. **Client Logos Slider (`<ClientLogosSlider />`)**
   - A dedicated carousel component displaying various client or partner logos.

4. **Stats / Delivery Section**
   - A section titled "Helping businesses grow smarter" which includes a description and a grid of key statistics (e.g., 15+ Years Industry Experience, 100+ Successful Digital Projects).

5. **Services / Help Grid Section (`<HelpSectionGrid />`)**
   - Titled "Strategy. Execution. Optimization." This displays a grid of cards detailing specific solutions:
     - Digital Presence & Web
     - Ecommerce Growth
     - AI & Automation
     - Search & Visibility
     - Performance Marketing
     - Data & Analytics

6. **AI Solutions / Production Loop Section (`<AiSection />`)**
   - A split-screen layout featuring a looping video on one side and interactive content on the other ("Powerful Solutions. Measurable Business Growth.") with auto-progressing tabs.

7. **Three Core Pillars Section**
   - A detailed layout outlining the three main pillars of the business: 
     - **Innovate**: AI Solutions
     - **Build**: Digital Engineering
     - **Scale**: Digital Growth & Analytics

8. **FAQ Section (`<FaqAccordion />`)**
   - An accordion-style section displaying Frequently Asked Questions.

9. **Contact Us Section**
   - The final section containing a comprehensive "Book Free Consultation" form alongside trust badges, tech stack icons (React, Next.js, Vercel, etc.), and contact information.
